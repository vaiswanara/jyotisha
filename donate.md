What does my contribution support?

Your contribution helps sustain both the learning platform and the ongoing work behind it.

Support received may be used for:

Internet and communication expenses
Electricity and utility costs
Software, hosting, and technical maintenance
Research and study resources
Development of new learning tools
Supporting the basic needs of the family while continuing this educational work

By helping meet these practical needs, you make it easier for this work to continue and for more knowledge to be developed and shared with students.

Why do you accept support if the project is offered freely?

e-Jyotisha is offered freely because knowledge should remain accessible to sincere learners.

At the same time, maintaining the platform, continuing research, creating new educational resources, and fulfilling the responsibilities of household life require time, effort, and basic material support.

Voluntary contributions help sustain both the work and the person performing the work, allowing the learning journey to continue without turning it into a commercial service.

Support is therefore received with gratitude, never as a fee, obligation, or requirement.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. Is e-Jyotisha free to use?

Yes.

e-Jyotisha is freely available for all sincere learners of Vedic Astrology. No payment is required to use the app.

2. Why is there a Support page if the app is free?

The Support page exists for those who feel that the project has been helpful and wish to contribute voluntarily.

Support is never expected, requested, or required.

3. Is support mandatory to access any feature?

No.

All learning resources and tools remain available regardless of whether someone chooses to contribute.

4. What does my contribution support?

Contributions help with:

Development and maintenance of the platform
Research and testing
Learning resources and documentation
Future improvements and new educational tools
5. Is this a commercial project?

No.

e-Jyotisha was created primarily as an educational initiative to support students of Jyotisha.

The purpose is learning and preservation of traditional knowledge.

6. Why do you describe support as a voluntary offering?

The platform follows the spirit of Amrita Vritti — receiving only what is willingly offered, without asking, expecting, or influencing others.

Support is accepted with gratitude, never with expectation.

7. Will I receive special access if I contribute?

No.

A contribution does not purchase privileges, special access, predictions, consultations, or priority support.

It is simply a voluntary expression of goodwill.

8. Will my name be displayed publicly?

No.

Contributions are treated respectfully and privately unless someone explicitly requests otherwise.

9. Can I support anonymously?

Yes.

If your chosen payment method allows it, anonymous support is welcome.

10. I cannot contribute financially. Can I still support the project?

Certainly.

You may support the project by:

Using the app sincerely
Reporting issues
Suggesting improvements
Helping other students
Sharing knowledge responsibly

Such support is equally valuable.

11. How much should I contribute?

There is no suggested amount.

Support, if offered, should arise from your own inner feeling and circumstances.

12. What if I choose not to contribute?

That is perfectly fine.

The app was created to serve learners, and you are welcome to continue using it freely.


14. Do you store my payment information?

No.

e-Jyotisha does not store your banking or payment details.

Any payment information is handled by the payment provider you choose to use.

15. What is the intention behind this project?

The intention is simple:

To make traditional Jyotisha learning accessible, practical, and freely available to sincere students.

Any support received helps sustain that effort.
